WHY ZARISHHEALTH

Good care should not depend on a perfect connection.

Many health teams work across rural, mobile or emergency settings. They need reliable records at the point of care, even when power, bandwidth and infrastructure are uncertain.

⌁

Work offline

Staff can continue registering people, recording consultations and managing services without an internet connection.

⌂

Keep control locally

Run the system on hardware you control. Your operational data does not need to pass through a vendor’s cloud.

↔

Connect when ready

Local devices can synchronise over Wi-Fi or an occasional uplink when a connection is available.

A SIMPLE MODEL

Each site can stand on its own.

The local site is the source of truth. Connectivity improves coordination, but it never becomes a condition for providing care.
